The conversation

The LLM: a statistical function

  • An LLM is one statistical function that understands a text and predicts what follows.
  • The input text is everything it knows about the task at hand.
  • Billions of parameters hold everything it learned from human text.
  • Chatbots, thinking models and agents are all built on this one function.
  • This chapter shows what “it predicts the next word” means exactly.
Demystifying LLMs 3

The conversation

Tokenization: encode text for statistics

  • All human text is encoded with one fixed vocabulary of about 256,000 tokens.
  • Tokens are frequent small slices of text rather than words.
  • A rare word is split into several frequent tokens.
Demystifying LLMs 4

The conversation

Inference: a whole document, one token at a time

The cat sat on the mat while the rain fell. It watched the drops race down the window, then curled up and fell asleep. When the storm passed, it woke, stretched and walked to the door, waiting for someone to let it out…
  • One call reads the whole text so far and predicts the next token.
  • The new token is added to the end of the text before the next call.
  • A whole document takes one call per token.
Demystifying LLMs 5

The conversation

Chatting: one turn at a time

  • Each time you send a message, the chatbot sends the whole conversation to the LLM.
  • The LLM writes the assistant’s turn token by token, until the end token.
  • The LLM remembers nothing between turns. The conversation it receives is its only memory.
  • A hidden first turn, the system prompt, gives the assistant its instructions.
Demystifying LLMs 6

The conversation

No learning in use: the parameters stay fixed

Monday, first conversation <user> My name is Léa, and I am planning a trip to Paris.<end> <assistant> Nice to meet you, Léa. When do you leave?<end>
Tuesday, new conversation <user> What is my name?<end> <assistant> I don’t know your name. You haven’t told me yet.<end>
  • Chatting changes no parameter. The LLM is the same after a conversation as before.
  • A new conversation starts from nothing.
Demystifying LLMs 7

The conversation

The context window: a hard limit on the conversation

  • The window is the most tokens the LLM can read at once.
  • Text outside the context window does not exist for the LLM.
  • Windows grew from a few thousand tokens in 2022 to a million today.
  • Older LLMs lost details as the window filled up. This was called context rot.
  • Recent LLMs use the whole window far better.

The conversation

Compaction: a summary replaces the conversation

<user> What is the capital of France?<end> <assistant> The capital of France is Paris, on the Seine.<end> … 300 more turns about hotels, trains and restaurants …
<user> Summary so far: a trip to Paris in May, Louvre first, hotel booked near the Seine, train from Lyon on the 12th.<end> <user> Which restaurant did we pick for Saturday?<end>
  • Near the limit, the chatbot asks the LLM to summarize the conversation.
  • The summary replaces the old turns, and the conversation continues from it.
  • A detail left out of the summary no longer exists for the LLM.
