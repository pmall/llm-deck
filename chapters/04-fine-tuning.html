Fine-tuning

The LLM: a statistical function

  • An LLM is one statistical function that understands a text and predicts what follows.
  • The input text is everything it knows about the task at hand.
  • Billions of parameters hold everything it learned from human text.
  • Chatbots, thinking models and agents are all built on this one function.
  • This chapter shows what “it predicts the next word” means exactly.
Demystifying LLMs 3

Fine-tuning

Tokenization: encode text for statistics

  • All human text is encoded with one fixed vocabulary of about 256,000 tokens.
  • Tokens are frequent small slices of text rather than words.
  • A rare word is split into several frequent tokens.

Demystifying LLMs 5

Fine-tuning

Inference: a whole document, one token at a time

The cat sat on the mat while the rain fell. It watched the drops race down the window, then curled up and fell asleep. When the storm passed, it woke, stretched and walked to the door, waiting for someone to let it out…
  • One call reads the whole text so far and predicts the next token.
  • The new token is added to the end of the text before the next call.
  • A whole document takes one call per token.

Fine-tuning

Emergent property: the LLM picks up patterns

What is the capital of Italy? Rome. What is the capital of Spain? Madrid. What is the capital of Japan? Tokyo. What is the capital of Turkey? Ankara. What is the capital of Egypt? Cairo. What is the capital of France? Paris.
What is a dish from Italy? 🍕 Pizza. What is a dish from Spain? 🥘 Paella. What is a dish from Japan? 🍣 Sushi. What is a dish from Turkey? 🥙 Kebab. What is a dish from Egypt? 🧆 Falafel. What is a dish from France? 🥐 Croissant.
  • The LLM spots patterns in the prompt and continues them.
  • This ability emerged on its own as LLMs grew larger.
  • Steering the LLM with a few examples is called few-shot prompting.
Demystifying LLMs 8

Fine-tuning

Fine-tuning: one concept takes the lead

  • Pre-training absorbs everything people wrote, as much as possible.
  • Fine-tuning picks one coherent set of documents and continues the training on it.
  • The LLM keeps its knowledge, but that set now steers how it writes.

Instruction tuning: from autocomplete to assistant

<user> What is the capital of France?<end> <assistant> The capital of France is Paris, on the Seine.<end> <user> What should I visit there first?<end> <assistant> The Louvre. Book ahead to see the Mona Lisa.<end>

  • Researchers manufactured millions of user–assistant conversations to train on.
  • Special tokens name who speaks and close each turn, like in a theatre script.
  • The LLM picks up the pattern and turns from an autocomplete into an assistant.
